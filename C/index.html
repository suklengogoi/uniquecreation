The studio of Uddip Phukan

We care for your emotions, we click for memories. Weddings, candid days , newborns, Bihu, Holi and the hills beyond .

UniqueCreation is a photography and films studio led by Uddip Phukan. Weddings are the heart of it, but the camera keeps going wherever there is a feeling worth keeping.

Every story is photographed and filmed with the same care: planned light, gentle direction and honest moments, delivered as photographs, films and reels.
